IDC Offering – App Modernization
--------------------------------
IDC offers a range of services for modernizing applications, including a comprehensive approach that addresses both the backend functionality and user experience. IDC helps move legacy workloads to a modern cloud platform and break down large, monolithic applications into smaller microservices, using modern methodologies such as DevOps, Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), and CI/CD as part of the modernization process.

IDC Technologies is well-versed in Agile-based deliveries. We will use an Agile/iterative approach to app modernization and project management (if required) that will help teams deliver value to their customers faster.

IDC's Services in App Modernization:
------------------------------------
**Microservices:**
IDC modernizes applications by implementing microservice patterns. This involves breaking down a monolithic application into smaller, independent "microservices." Each microservice is a small, specialized unit of functionality, making it easier to maintain and update, compared to a large monolithic application. Additionally, microservices can be scaled individually, rather than scaling the entire application, resulting in more efficient resource utilization and improved performance.

**DevOps:**
IDC implements DevOps automation by utilizing tools such as Jenkins or Terraform to automate repetitive tasks such as testing, deployment, and infrastructure provisioning. This enables organizations to quickly and dependably roll out new features. IDC also employs advanced deployment techniques like Rolling updates, Canary Releases, and Blue-Green to achieve zero downtime during deployment.

**Containers:**
IDC modernizes applications by utilizing a containerization approach, which allows them to run on any platform that supports containerization technology, without concerns about dependencies or system configuration. IDC employs various container runtimes such as Docker, and CIR-O, as well as multiple container orchestration tools such as Kubernetes and Openshift.

**Data Modernization:**
IDC modernizes data for both SQL and No-SQL databases by synchronizing, replicating, and transferring data. We ensure that replication on the platform continues to function seamlessly after migration. IDC also migrates data from on-premises RDBMS to Cloud RDBMS and performs transformations from RDBMS to No-SQL.
